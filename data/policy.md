# Fictional PMO Governance Policy (DEMO ONLY)
Policy ID: PMO-2026-01. Owner: Portfolio Governance Office.

1. Escalate a project when its approved budget forecast exceeds approved budget by MORE THAN 10%.
2. Escalate a project when a milestone is overdue by MORE THAN 14 calendar days and is not marked complete.
3. Escalate a project when status reporting is older than 14 calendar days.
4. Missing sponsor or project manager requires data-quality remediation, not automatic high-risk classification.
5. Critical priority plus an unresolved blocker requires management review.
6. No agent may change a project, contact a person, or create an escalation task without a human approval action.
7. For conflicting information, request human verification and cite the project ID and applicable policy rule.
8. All demo data is synthetic; findings must not be represented as real Butterball or other company information.
